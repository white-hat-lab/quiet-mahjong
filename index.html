How to play

Tap two matching tiles in the same row or column, with no tiles between them, to clear the pair. Diagonal matches and paths that turn do not count.

Drag any tile up, down, left or right. Tiles in front of it move together into available space. If a moved tile lines up with its match along a clear row or column, the pair disappears automatically. If no match is made, the whole group returns to its starting spots. Tiles cannot be pushed beyond the board edge.
